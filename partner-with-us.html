The Process

From the day you sign up to the day your first guest checks in, we handle everything.

Step 01 · The First Look

We get to know your villa

Before a single photo is taken, we sit with the space — light, layout, what makes it worth booking — and build a plan around it, not a template.

  • On-site visit with the owner
  • Photography and listing assets scoped
  • Ideal guest profile defined
  • Go-live checklist agreed together
Step 02 · Go Live

We set the price and put it out there

The right platform, the right price, the right first impression — set and re-set as the season moves.

  • Channels matched to the property
  • Seasonal rate calendar built and maintained
  • Copy and gallery polished for conversion
  • Performance watched closely after go-live
Step 03 · The Stay

Your guests are looked after, always

From the first enquiry to the last checkout, you're never the one picking up the phone at 11pm.

  • Enquiry and booking desk
  • Check-in and on-ground house support
  • Issues resolved locally, in real time
  • Checkout and turnover handled end to end
Step 04 · The Payoff

Your income lands, on time, every month

No hidden cuts, no vague reporting. A simple split, paid on time, with the numbers to back it up.

70:30

You keep 70% of every booking. We take 30% — and only once the property is actually earning.

  • Monthly owner payouts
  • Transparent performance notes
  • Regular occupancy and rate reviews
  • Incentives that stay aligned with yours
