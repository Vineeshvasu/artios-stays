About Artios

One woman, one villa, and a refusal to let it sit empty.

Boutique stays along two coastlines, still run the way they started — personally.

Founder

Handing over your home to strangers shouldn't feel like a leap of faith.

Artios was built from a single villa and a background in real estate that had shown exactly how most property management actually worked: built for spreadsheets, not for the people living in the space or the owner who trusted it to someone else.

So it was built differently — on-ground teams who know each property by name, a reservations desk that actually answers, and a founder who still reads the reviews herself.

Years later, Artios manages homes across two coastlines. The size has changed. The way it's run hasn't.

"I'd rather answer every guest message myself than let a home I care about become just another listing nobody's watching."

Shweta, Founder & CEO

The Artios Way

What we actually do differently.

01
